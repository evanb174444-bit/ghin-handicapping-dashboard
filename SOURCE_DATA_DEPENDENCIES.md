# Remaining Source-Data Dependencies

Phase 1 uses clearly labeled deterministic mock data. Phase 2 requires approved source extracts and business definitions for:

- Central Computation active Handicap Index golfers by month and all five segmentation dimensions
- GHIN active Handicap Index golfers by month and the same dimensions
- AGA and GHIN score totals, channel, and score-entry detail
- GHIN App, ghin.com, and kiosk posting attribution
- Enhanced GPS subscriber snapshots, first-year upgrades, renewals, and cancellations
- Central Computation provider score volume and provider mapping history
- Association master data, stable identifiers, and association-to-region mapping
- Active app user, repeat-engagement, and product-adoption definitions
- Official reporting calendar, YTD cutoff rules, late-arriving-data policy, and reconciliation tolerances
- Valid-value mapping tables, including Unknown and Unmapped handling

The original spreadsheet workbook and official raw exports were not included in the supplied package. They must remain outside the published site and feed a validated processing pipeline.
